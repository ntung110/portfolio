## Write before it feels finished

Keeping notes changes the way I learn. Explaining an idea makes the gaps more obvious, and it gives me something concrete to return to when the details start to fade.

## Small notes count

Not every post needs to be a complete guide. A short explanation of one decision, a failed experiment, or a useful reference can be enough to help someone else.

## A record, not a performance

This is a sample journal entry. Use this space for ideas you are exploring and work you are still figuring out. A personal blog can be a notebook rather than a finished statement.
