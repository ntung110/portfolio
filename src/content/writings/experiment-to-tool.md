## Look beyond the demo

An experiment answers whether an idea might work. A useful tool has to answer a few more questions: who needs it, how it behaves when things go wrong, and whether the result is worth the effort.

## Build the feedback loop

I like to test the most uncertain assumption early. A little observation often tells me more than another feature. That feedback helps decide what to keep, what to simplify, and what to leave behind.
