## A small problem worth solving

This example explores a simple idea: a personal tool that turns scattered notes into a short, useful daily plan. Rather than adding more features, the goal is to make one everyday task easier to start.

## Starting with the essentials

The sample workflow has three steps: collect a note, decide what matters, and choose the next action. A small scope leaves room to focus on clear language, predictable behavior, and a straightforward interface.

## What I would test next

The next step would be trying the workflow with real notes and observing where it adds friction. This is an illustrative project story, ready to replace with your own screenshots, implementation details, and results.
